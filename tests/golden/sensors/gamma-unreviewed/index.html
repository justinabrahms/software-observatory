Gamma Unreviewed

Behavioral Low oracle Retrospective

Gamma Unreviewed deliberately carries no last_reviewed date, no see_also, no categories, no references, and none of the optional *_note fields. It is the honest-provenance path: an entry nobody has re-read since it was written must render as “not yet re-reviewed” rather than borrowing a date from somewhere convenient.

It is also the minimum-frontmatter case, so every optional-field branch in the sensor page renderer is exercised in its absent form by at least one fixture.

Navigating the signals
that software emits.

Software is not silent. Every system, at every layer, continuously emits evidence of what it is doing — logs, metrics, traces, events, states, failures, recoveries. The Software Observatory is a field guide to that evidence: what to look for, where it appears, how to read it, and what it tells you about the health, behavior, and intent of the systems you depend on.

P99 Latency Divergence

When the 99th-percentile response time diverges sharply from the median, the system is telling you something specific: a small number of requests are hitting a pathological path. This signal appears in virtually every request-processing system, yet is frequently drowned out by aggregate averages.
